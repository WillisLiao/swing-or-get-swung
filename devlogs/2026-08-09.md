## L4 blueprint-authoritative Concourse rebuild - accepted

The user rejected the previous candidate for missing blueprint texture and industrial surface language, wrong colours, darkness, missing lime objective glow, almost no visible detail, an unrecognisable 3/4 overview, and invisible walls that made movement frustrating.

Both MCP gates were established before investigation: Godot MCP through the connected editor plugin, Blender MCP through the addon socket.

### Root cause

`scripts/riftline_map.gd` only builds visible meshes when the authored GLB *fails* to load.
Since the GLB always loads, live runtime inspection found 129 collision `StaticBody3D` nodes and exactly 0 of them carrying any visible mesh.
The GLB had also been authored as an eight-way radial composition while the collision contract is a two-base concourse, so visible geometry and collision described different maps.
Every collidable surface in the shipping build was therefore an invisible wall.

The second root cause was material, not geometry.
`CONCRETE_Shell` `#343a38` (Y=0.0404) against `STEEL_Structure` `#303634` (Y=0.0352) is a 1.06:1 luminance ratio, so 11,752 of the shell's 18,484 triangles of authored panel work were painted the same value as the surface behind them.
`NuclearMaterials.clean_surface` then hard-set `AO = 1.0` and zeroed every detail term, which removed the only contact darkening available on a shadowless map with no screen-space AO.

### Approach

The shell is now generated directly from `RiftlineMapLayout`'s 129 solids, dumped to JSON by the new `tools/riftline_map_layout_dump.gd` and read by the Blender build.
Geometry and collision cannot disagree by construction.
`tools/riftline_map_visual_exercise.gd` gained `_assert_every_solid_is_visible`, which fails the build if any gameplay solid has no authored geometry over it.

`shaders/nuclear_pbr.gdshader` gained an analytic, derivative-antialiased pattern set - floor plates, wall panels with corner bolts, scratched steel, grate slots, 45-degree hazard striping, light-strip housings and concrete mottle - plus restored material AO.
It later gained a hemisphere term and a contact-darkening term to give hard-surface geometry its form back without shadows.

`shaders/nuclear_sky.gdshader` is a new analytic sky: stars in two layers, one galactic band, and a warm horizon lift from the facility's own fixtures.
No green appears in it, because nuclear-lime is reserved for the objective.

The shell carries nine roles rather than eight.
The blueprint's lighting sheet separates OBJECTIVE FOCUS lime from LANDMARK LIGHTING lime, and while they shared a role the decorative containment inserts bloomed exactly as hard as the reactor and out-massed it inside its own arena.

### Collision and movement fixes

`OverlookInnerParapetNorth` walled off 2.25m of the northern ramp's 4.5m exit and was pulled clear.
The `overlook` lane waypoint at `(11.4, 3.6, 0.3)` sat 0.47m off the upper connector behind a guard rail; it now sits at `(15.4, 3.55, 4.2)`, clear of both the sightline break at the connector's midpoint and the central bridge rails at its low end.
The outer-wall plinth kerb protruded 0.64m inward past the wall's collision - visible geometry with nothing solid behind it - and was cut to 0.26m.
The solid count remains 129.

### Review

Five bounded read-only visual reviews ran. `Sol` and `Terra` are not agent types in this harness, so the read-only `frontend-visual-reviewer` at Opus filled both roles; it never edited files or operated MCP.
The preflight verdict was FAIL on all fourteen criteria.
Acceptance rounds returned NOT FAITHFUL four times - on black clipping, on a cyan cast, on missing bloom, on a single-tier lime, and finally on a lime letterform at the core - before returning PASS on the fifth.

Two of the reviewer's refutations were of my own measurements, and both were correct: a `G>200` lime discriminator swept 7,482 accent pixels into the objective tier, and a replacement `b < g*0.62` mask excluded the bloomed near-white pixels entirely.

### Measurement correction

`editor_manage(monitors_get)` reports the editor process, not the running game.
Hiding the entire map shell does not move its reading.
The 320, 327 and 328 draw-call figures recorded in earlier sessions were never measuring the game.
Measured inside the running game by toggling shell visibility, the map shell costs 9 draw calls and 27,568 primitives, and a player-height pose totals 219 draw calls.

### Validation

Headless import clean; all 19 `tools/*_exercise.gd` pass; 129 solids and 129 static bodies; shell 9 meshes with 0 shadow casters and 0 colliders; mobile renderer with both lights shadowless; headless CPU sample `frames=120 avg_ms=16.67 min_ms=16.67 max_ms=16.67`.
Live traversal reaches the upper deck on all four ramps, ending at `(30.92, 3.20, 14.0)` and mirrored positions.
Corridor black clipping fell from 0.337 to 0.025 and the neutral colour cast from B/R 2.05 to 0.84-0.93 against the blueprint's 0.83-0.85.
Facility median luminance is 2.9x the sky's, so the composition is not inverted.

Headless mode uses a dummy rendering driver and measures no GPU cost.
The added per-fragment work is bounded - roughly 8 ALU on map surfaces for the shading terms, and an early-rejected star lattice on sky pixels - with no new draw call, geometry, light, render pass or per-frame CPU work, but only the device settles mobile GPU cost.
