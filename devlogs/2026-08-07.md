# 2026-08-07 - Nuclear Rush: Robert's map becomes the only map, 4v4, realistic PBR

## What landed

**Robert's circular arena is now the game's only map.**
Ported forward from PR #1 (`robertwu072792`, branch `circular-nuke-arena`) rather than merged, because that branch predates two commits on `main`: the SUN/VOID to RED/BLUE rename and the lean removal.
His layout dimensions are preserved exactly - 60m radius floor, 40 tangent wall segments, base walls at z=+/-55 with side walls at x=+/-13, the 16-segment centre pickup room at radius 8.2, base cover pairs at z=+/-43, four cover columns per side at x=+/-32 (z=-24/-8/8/24), shoulder blocks at (+/-25, +/-34), three low round covers per side at z=+/-15, raised mid decks at z=+/-29 with ramps and supports, and roofed flank underpasses at x=+/-42.
His `_route_nodes` graph and lane posts were copied verbatim.
`RiftlineMap.Id` collapsed to the single value `CONCOURSE`; DUEL_YARD and all its geometry are gone.
RED holds north (+Z), BLUE holds south (-Z).

**Nuclear Rush is the only game mode**, implemented to `handoffs/DESIGN-nuclear-rush.md`.
Deathmatch and bomb defuse were deleted entirely: no `BombState`, no rounds, no side swaps, no intermission.
The core is carried to the carrying team's **own** pad - this is the correction to PR #1, which delivered to the enemy base.

Rules as built: 4v4, one continuous 600s clock, one core at centre, 2.5s install hold on your own pad, 25s launch countdown visible to both teams, 3s enemy hold-to-cancel, 1 point per successful launch only, first to 3 wins, higher score wins at expiry, tie enters **unbounded** sudden death, carrier death drops the core for either team, dropped core returns to centre after 15s, core respawns at centre 4s after a launch or cancel, carrier moves at 0.82x with full loadout, 3.0s respawn delay.

**The six open questions in the design doc are now decided** and recorded in it: movement-only carrier penalty (no sidearm lock), roles as playstyle labels only, 25s countdown visible to both teams, one fixed pad per base, hold-to-cancel matching the existing interact pattern, and unbounded sudden death.

That last one is also the deliberate fix for the stalemate tie-break raised in review on PR #1: the old `_finish_nuke_timeout()` fell through to `winner = nuke_carrier_team`, which resolved to whichever team the variable was initialised with when nobody had ever carried the core. An unbounded sudden death removes the accidental default rather than papering over it with a coin flip.

**Session layer is 4v4 and has nothing left to negotiate.**
`PROTOCOL_VERSION` 9 -> 10 (deliberate wire break). `RiftlineRoster.MAX_TEAM_SIZE` 5 -> 4, default team size 4. `map_id` and `game_mode` removed from the session descriptor, the lobby-state whitelist, and every adoption path; only display labels `MODE_LABEL`/`MAP_LABEL` remain, and they are never parsed back into an enum. `arena_id`, `arena_override_set`, `game_mode`, `default_arena_for_team_size()`, `arena_name()`, `arena_id_from_name()` all deleted. `RiftlineLobby.configure()` lost its `arena_id` parameter. Host capacity is now a single `APP_HOST_REMOTE_SLOTS := 7` so a full 4v4 including the host fits. The practice panel's DEATHMATCH/BOMB selector and its `mode_requested` signal are gone, remaining buttons re-flowed. `--mode=` and the map-override CLI options are gone.

**Realistic art direction, still with zero imported art.**
New `shaders/nuclear_pbr.gdshader`: real metal-roughness response (`diffuse_burley`, `specular_schlick_ggx`, no custom `light()`), procedural surface relief whose normal comes from an **analytic** sine-sum gradient, isotropic value-noise grime pooling in recesses, dust on upward faces, and material-supplied AO to substitute for the screen-space AO the Mobile renderer does not have.
The analytic gradient was chosen over noise-based normals specifically because perturbing from noise needs three extra samples per fragment at 120 Hz.
New `scripts/nuclear_materials.gd` (`NuclearMaterials`) is the single material factory - `concrete`, `metal`, `painted_metal`, `polymer`, `marking`, `emissive` - with parameter-keyed caching, because the arena builds hundreds of meshes and each distinct `ShaderMaterial` costs a uniform buffer.
Arena environment rebuilt: `ProceduralSkyMaterial` sky, `AMBIENT_SOURCE_SKY` + `REFLECTION_SOURCE_SKY` (the sky reflections are what make the PBR metal read as metal), `TONE_MAPPER_ACES`, glow, depth fog, adjustments, and a shadowed directional key with `SHADOW_PARALLEL_4_SPLITS` and a soft angular distance.
`project.godot` gained 4K/2K directional and positional shadow atlases, anisotropic filtering, 2x MSAA, and `force_vertex_shading=false`.

The renderer stays **Mobile**. SSAO, SSIL, SDFGI, SSR and volumetric fog are Forward+ only and are not affordable at 120 Hz on a phone; the arena is grepped clean of all five.

**Naming.** The game is **Swing or Get Swung**; `application/config/name` is the short form **SOGS** because iOS truncates a long label under the icon. Applied across `project.godot`, `export_presets.cfg`, `deploy.sh`, and the product-name exercise. Bundle id stays `com.lull.riftline` on purpose - changing it breaks code signing and orphans existing installs. The GitHub repo is now `WillisLiao/swing-or-get-swung`.

## Bug fixed: settings panel touch capture

Reported: pressing a settings slider and sliding - without lifting - onto another slider or button activated the other control and "messed up the whole settings".

Root cause was `duel_hud.gd`'s input dispatch replaying every `InputEventScreenDrag` as a fresh **press** (`_handle_settings_touch(index, position, true)`), so the handler re-ran its entire top-to-bottom hit test at the new position. Worse than the report suggested: the toggle chips ran `_aim_toggle = not _aim_toggle` on *every* drag event, flipping many times a second, and sliding off the panel edge closed the panel. `_settings_owner_touch` looked like it should have prevented this but tracks the combat HUD's settings *button*, not the control a settings touch has captured, and is normally -1 once the panel is open.

Fix: per-touch capture. `_settings_captures` maps a touch index to the control id it captured on press. `_settings_control_at()` is now the single place a point resolves to a control. Presses resolve once and apply; `_handle_settings_drag()` ignores any index without a capture and routes only to the captured id, where `_apply_settings_drag()` handles the three sliders and no-ops everything else; release erases only its own index. Tap-outside-to-close fires only for a press whose own down event landed outside. The mouse path routes identically.

`tools/riftline_settings_touch_exercise.gd` is a new regression test that drives real `InputEventScreenTouch`/`InputEventScreenDrag` objects through `_gui_input()`: 16 assertions covering the reported gesture, that sliders still drag their own control, that chips toggle exactly once per press and never per drag frame, that a captured drag leaving the panel does not close it, and that an unmatched touch index is ignored.

## Session 2: multiplayer hardening and three reported bugs

The user asked for a full multiplayer/FPS-architecture review before weapons work, using the `sonnet-opus` skill.
Inspection found the existing net layer already server-authoritative and reasonably solid: ENet client-server, protocol-versioned handshake, input validation with sequence/turn-rate clamping, client-side prediction with server reconciliation, and a network-condition simulator for testing latency/jitter/loss.
The user confirmed the goal was hardening that LAN/dedicated-server model as a foundation for later internet play, not building internet play itself this session, and scoped this session to net hardening plus the three reported bugs only - weapons, class/loadout redesign, and the art/UI redesign all got their own `handoffs/NEXT-SESSION-*.md` bootstrap files instead.

### Reconnect grace period

The concrete gap found: `RiftlineLobby.remove_peer()` abandoned the entire match the instant *any* human's connection dropped during LIVE/ARMING, and `admit_peer()` only worked during STAGING - so a mobile Wi-Fi/cellular blip was both an instant loss for the other seven players and permanently un-rejoinable for the player who dropped.

Added a token-based reconnect flow:
- `RiftlineRoster` now issues a `rejoin_token` per human admission, and gained `disconnect_peer()` (reserve the slot, mark `connected: false`, keep the token) alongside the existing `remove_peer()` (free the slot immediately, used pre-match where there is no match state worth preserving).
- `RiftlineLobby` gained `disconnect_peer()` (keeps phase LIVE instead of abandoning), `reclaim_peer(token, peer_id)`, and `sweep_grace(now_msec)` - a 20-second grace window (`RECONNECT_GRACE_MS`) before an unreclaimed slot is permanently removed and the match is abandoned, same as before.
- `riftline_network.gd`: a peer connecting while the match is LIVE/ARMING no longer gets admitted as new; it must present a `rejoin` client event with its token (sent automatically by the client the instant it reconnects, from a token it received in the original `assigned_actor` event and which survives `stop()`). A bad or missing token still gets rejected exactly as before.
- New regression coverage appended to `tools/riftline_lobby_exercise.gd`: disconnect-during-LIVE keeps the match LIVE, a wrong token cannot steal the reserved slot, the right token restores actor identity and team under a new peer id, and a fully-expired grace window still abandons the match.

Two follow-ups landed later the same session: a mid-match connection that never presents a token (a stranger, or a client that isn't reconnect-aware) is now tracked in `_pending_rejoin_peers` and dropped after `PENDING_REJOIN_TIMEOUT_MS` (5s) instead of sitting idle forever; and a client that loses its connection now auto-redials the address it was last connected to every `AUTO_RECONNECT_RETRY_MS` (1.5s) for as long as its rejoin token is still inside the server's grace window, via `_tick_auto_reconnect()`. A voluntary leave (`submit_client_lobby_leave()`) clears the token and cancels any pending auto-reconnect so it can't undo an intentional exit.

### Bug 1: health bar was 5 chunks, not 100 HP

`duel_hud.gd:_draw_vitality_strip` rendered `clampi(ceili(health / 20.0), 0, 5)` as five discrete plates - a "5 hits and you're dead" display - even though `duelist.gd`'s underlying model was already a real `HEALTH := 100.0` float, confirmed by the ballistics comment "23 damage means five body hits to eliminate a 100 HP duelist" and by `take_damage()`. This was a display bug only. Replaced with a continuous 0-100 fill bar plus a numeric HP readout.

### Bug 2: shots not registering at range

`rift_ballistics.gd`'s `M4_MAX_RANGE` was 48 meters on a `CONCOURSE_RADIUS := 60.0` (120m diameter) map - a projectile crossing the open core room or a long base-to-base sightline simply vanished mid-flight with zero damage and no impact event once it traveled 48m, which is exactly what "no matter how many bullets land they don't register" at distance looks like from the receiving end. There was no damage falloff at all previously - damage was a flat 23 regardless of distance up to the (too-short) cutoff.

Fixed with a real falloff curve instead of a hard vanish: full damage (23) inside 22m, linear falloff to a 14-damage floor by 70m, and range raised to 95m so a shot that reaches a target inside the arena always registers, just for less as distance increases. `_damage_for_distance()` is the new single source of truth; `_handle_impact()` now computes actual travel distance from `source_position` to the hit point rather than assuming a fixed value.

### Bug 3: bullet and impact visuals were a literal cross

`riftline_arena.gd`'s projectile presentation pool built the in-flight tracer as a thin `BoxMesh` streak, and - the actual reported bug - the wall/duelist impact effect as two perpendicular boxes (`vertical`/`horizontal`), i.e. a literal `+` on every hit, plus a ring. Tracer is now a small sphere with a slight directional stretch (reads as a fast round without becoming a bar/line); the impact mark is now a flush circular scorch decal plus the existing expanding ring - an actual bullet mark, not a cross. Both `_spawn_projectile_impact` and the reused `_show_capture_impact` path (same pool) were updated for the new 2-child (mark, ring) structure.

## Third session: weapons, accuracy model, and class loadouts

Implemented `handoffs/NEXT-SESSION-weapons-and-loadouts.md` in full - a `/sonnet-opus` session, with `opus-advisor` consulted before finalizing the accuracy-state design and weapon data schema (large, long-lived gameplay contract; cross-cutting into the networked input/snapshot model).

### Iron sights and the knife are gone

Every weapon now ADS's through an optic/scope presentation. `Duelist.shot_origin_for()`/`optic_tip_head_offset()` replace the old PULSE-only `ads_iron_sight_tip_*` code, driven per-weapon by a new `optic_tip_local`/`ads_anchor` pair in the weapon table. There is no dedicated melee weapon: `Duelist.melee_attack()` swings whichever of the five weapons is currently equipped, with its own range/damage/cooldown/anim-tag pulled from that weapon's row. The `knife_strike` signal, `KNIFE_*` constants, and the `Weapon.KNIFE` enum value are gone; `melee_strike`/`melee_struck` replace them end to end (arena, combat feedback, the wire event).

### Five real weapons, one data table

`scripts/rift_weapons.gd` is a new `const Dictionary` (deliberately not a `Resource` - it's read inside the deterministic, replayable simulation path on every peer and must be byte-identical, and a preloaded Resource is a shared mutable object) holding all five weapons: assault rifle, MP7-reference SMG, S1897-reference pump shotgun (9 pellets in a fixed rotated rosette, not independent draws - a learnable pattern instead of pure variance), a 9mm-class pistol (not a revolver/hand-cannon), and a Halo-Infinite-referenced sniper (two zoom steps, 0.38s ADS time, zero ADS drift by construction - every `ads_*` accuracy term is literally 0 in its row). `scripts/rift_ballistics.gd` is now fully data-driven off that table instead of hardcoded `M4_*` constants, including per-projectile falloff scalars copied at spawn (a projectile must not read the shooter's *current* weapon at impact, since they may have swapped) and multi-pellet support sharing one `shot_id`.

### Accuracy model

New `Duelist` state - `bloom` (continuous, decays), `shot_counter` (monotonic, never resets, indexes the pellet-dispersion hash), `ads_progress` (ramps over the weapon's `ads_seconds`) - composes into a cone half-angle via `RiftWeapons.cone_for(weapon, speed_t, crouched, airborne, ads_progress, bloom)`, covering stationary/moving/jumping/crouching x hipfire/ADS for every weapon from one formula rather than a discrete per-state table (which has no defined answer for combined states like crouch-strafing mid-ADS). Airborne is derived from `velocity.y` (which is snapshotted), not `is_on_floor()` (which isn't). Pellet/shot dispersion is sampled with a stateless integer hash keyed on `(actor_id.hash(), shot_counter, pellet_index)` - never a `RandomNumberGenerator`, whose internal state isn't part of the network snapshot. `bloom`/`shot_counter`/`ads_progress` are in both `authoritative_state()` and `apply_presentation_state()` (verified symmetric by script) - omitting either one was the single highest-risk failure mode opus-advisor flagged, since it would present as "hit registration feels bad" rather than a visible error.

### Four classes, loadout slots

`Duelist.PlayerClass` (frontline/sniper/runner/shield) resolves through `CLASS_LOADOUTS` into 1-2 weapon slots: frontline picks AR/SMG/shotgun for slot 1 plus a pistol; sniper is sniper+pistol; runner and shield are pistol-only. Runner wears the nuclear vest (removes the new carry damage-over-time, keeps the existing 0.82x speed penalty); shield carries a Montagne-reference frontal plate that blocks full damage - bullet or melee - arriving from within roughly a 120-degree frontal arc, no degrade (a defensible v1 simplification, recorded rather than silently chosen). Carrying the core without the vest now costs `CORE_CARRY_DAMAGE_PER_SECOND` (2.5 HP/s) on top of the speed penalty. Ammo is tracked per slot (`_slot_magazine`/`_slot_reserve`) and restored on swap; swapping slots (or, while ADS'd on the sniper, cycling its two zoom steps instead) carries a 0.35s lockout so it can't be used to reset fire timing for free. `RiftlineRoster` gained `player_class`/`primary_weapon` fields and `set_class()`; there is deliberately no selection *screen* yet (that's the art/UI redesign session's job), so every actor defaults to Frontline/Rifle today, but the full data contract and `Duelist.configure_loadout()` wiring already work end to end for a future picker or dedicated-server config to drive.

### Weapon visuals

Mid-session the user flagged the existing single-carbine model as reading "Roblox/TF2-like" rather than the realistic Halo/Destiny register the rest of the game (`nuclear_pbr` + `NuclearMaterials`) already commits to. Rebuilt all five view models on `NuclearMaterials.metal/polymer/emissive` instead of the old flat-shaded `pulp_lit` boxes, with tapered cylinders and angled parts per archetype (bullpup-length SMG, exposed-tube-magazine pump shotgun, raked-grip pistol, long free-floated sniper barrel with a turreted scope) rather than uniform box stacks - still "lightly on the model side" per the handoff's explicit scope (silhouette differentiation, not final art), but a clear step up from the placeholder geometry.

### Networking

`PROTOCOL_VERSION` bumped 10 -> 11: a new `melee` input field (validated the same way as `fire`, and - like the knife before it - deliberately *not* locally predicted on a joining client, since it has no self-guarding authority check the way `RiftBallistics.fire()` does), the renamed `melee_strike` wire event, and the new snapshot fields above. Mismatched builds refuse to pair rather than silently desync.

## Fourth session: main menu, class picker, manual respawn, and two bug fixes

User-reported after the weapons/loadouts session: no way to actually pick a class, the settings panel's MAIN MENU button went nowhere, and health displayed 0 after respawning until the next hit landed. Also requested: respawn should no longer be instant - a 5-second minimum with a manual respawn button, so there's somewhere for the class re-pick to live.

### Health display bug

`duel_hud.gd`'s `health` field was only ever updated by the `damaged` signal handler (fires on damage) or, in the LAN path, a per-tick `hud.show_damage(_local_duelist.health)` sync. The **offline** path (solo/drill practice, and the host's own view) never had that per-tick sync, so `Duelist.respawn_at()` silently resetting `health` back to 100 was invisible to the HUD until the player's next hit. Added the same per-tick sync to both offline branches in `riftline_arena.gd`.

### Manual respawn instead of instant auto-respawn

`RiftlineMatch` used to `await get_tree().create_timer(3.0).timeout` and respawn automatically. Replaced with a per-actor elapsed-time dict (`_respawn_elapsed`) ticked every `_physics_process`, `RESPAWN_MIN_SECONDS := 5.0`, and three new methods: `can_respawn(actor_id)`, `respawn_seconds_remaining(actor_id)`, `request_respawn(actor_id)` - the last one the *only* thing that actually respawns someone, and it's a no-op before the minimum elapses or once already alive (self-guarding, safe to call repeatedly).

### Class picker (new: `scripts/riftline_class_panel.gd`)

A reusable custom-drawn `Control` (same idiom as the existing practice/rift-link panels) showing the four classes plus a primary-weapon sub-row for Frontline, with a context-driven CTA button. Reused in two places:
- **Pre-game**, wired from a new main menu (below).
- **Death screen**: shown automatically the instant the local player's own duelist becomes eliminated, hidden the instant they're alive again. The CTA doubles as the respawn button, disabled with a countdown until `RiftlineMatch.can_respawn()` says otherwise. Changing class while dead applies immediately via `Duelist.configure_loadout()` (safe - an eliminated duelist isn't rendering combat), so it's already correct by the time the actual respawn happens.

For a non-host LAN client, the pending class/respawn state rides the existing per-tick input frame (same channel `fire`/`melee`/`aim` already use) via three new fields - `respawn`, `class_id`, `primary_weapon` - validated in `riftline_network.gd` and applied host-side per remote actor. **Protocol bumped 11 -> 12.**

While wiring this, found and fixed a real pre-existing bug from the weapons session: `riftline_arena.gd`'s `_continuous_input()` (the function that reconstructs a remote peer's per-tick state on the host) never copied the `melee` field through, so **remote players' melee attacks never actually worked over LAN** - only locally. Fixed alongside the new fields.

### Main menu (new: `scripts/riftline_main_menu.gd`)

Three top-level actions - CREATE GAME, JOIN GAME, ENTER DRILL - each handing off to the class picker before actually doing anything. This is now the real app entry point (previously the practice-drill panel was, with no "create/join" presented at the same level) and, more importantly, the actual destination of the settings panel's MAIN MENU button, which is what was "going nowhere": `_on_rift_link_cancelled()`'s offline branch used to just hide an already-hidden panel and do nothing, and its LAN branch silently dropped the player into a *new* offline solo match instead of returning to any menu. Both branches now funnel through a rewritten `_restore_offline_training()` that tears down whatever match/connection state exists and shows the main menu.

The old drill-picker panel (solo/wing/full squad size) is no longer reachable from the new flow - ENTER DRILL goes straight to the class panel and starts the real 4v4 (bots fill both sides), which is arguably the more representative practice mode anyway. The panel's code and its capture/preview CLI plumbing are untouched, since tooling still depends on it.

### Optic visuals

User follow-up: despite the weapon rework, ADS still looked like iron sights - the five weapon models had a small glowing accent box near the sight position, but nothing read as glass to look *through*. Added `Duelist._add_optic_lens()`, a small glowing lens disc placed at each weapon's exact `optic_tip_local` calibration point - the same point `Duelist.optic_tip_head_offset()` already uses for the ADS shot origin - so the lens lands precisely where the camera centers while aiming, on every weapon.

### Verification

Headless import clean. All 16 `tools/*_exercise.gd` pass, plus a new respawn-gate block appended to `riftline_modes_exercise.gd` (eliminated -> can't respawn yet -> timer -> eligible but still requires a request -> respawns -> immediately ineligible again). Screenshot capture tooling itself is non-functional in this sandbox (dummy rendering driver, confirmed by testing a known-good pre-existing preview flag - not a regression), so the menu/class-panel/optic-lens layouts were verified by code review and an on-device deploy rather than a capture diff.

Also found and fixed, unrelated to the above: `project.godot` never registered a `melee` input action even though the weapons session wired `Input.is_action_just_pressed("melee")` into `riftline_arena.gd` - every physics tick threw an engine error. Bound to the V key.

### Not done this session

Drill squad-size selection (solo/wing/full) is no longer reachable from the main menu (see above - scope simplification, not an oversight). The offline 4v4 bots previously used `BotDuelist` only for target acquisition, strafing, and firing. Although `RiftlineArena` already sent an objective context dictionary, the bot never consumed it and therefore ignored the core, launch pads, installs, and cancels.

`BotDuelist` now assigns one of four deterministic squad roles: Runner, Escort, Defender, or Raider. Its objective plan is recomputed from the authoritative match context. A carrier routes to its own launch pad and holds interact to install; teammates escort the carrier or prepare the pad; enemies intercept a carrier; a friendly installed core is defended; and an enemy installed core is raided and cancelled with the same held-interact path used by a human. With a neutral or dropped core, the roles split between claiming it, screening the runner, holding the home lane, and contesting the center. Respawn resets formation rather than preserving a stale target.

Movement uses `RiftlineMap.route_toward()` so the new decisions follow the existing navigation nodes around procedural cover. Combat remains opportunistic: a bot can engage a visible enemy while continuing toward its strategic destination. Deterministic formation offsets, short side-steps, jumping, and stuck recovery keep teammates from collapsing onto one point. `RiftlineArena` now assigns roles after the 4v4 roster is built, continuously refreshes carried-core positions, and forwards each bot's interaction intent into `RiftlineMatch`.

Added `tools/riftline_bot_objective_exercise.gd`. It verifies the role plans for neutral, carried, and installed states, then runs the real match rules through bot pickup, home-pad install, and opposing-team cancel. Godot 4.7.1 headless import is clean and all 17 exercises pass. A live MCP 4v4 run confirmed all seven offline bots spawned and changed positions under their objective plans; the game framebuffer and HUD rendered normally and the current game log contained no errors.

## Fourteenth session: High Alert chip v1 (Robert, PR #8, merged after the thirteenth session above)

Added the requested COD-like High Alert chip as a universal local passive for the first version. `scripts/riftline_high_alert.gd` tracks each enemy independently and only qualifies a threat after 0.5 seconds when the enemy is alive, on the opposing team, at least 55% into ADS, within 95m, aiming within a 5.5-degree cone at the local player, outside the local camera frustum, and unobstructed by layer-1 world collision. The check runs entirely against local presentation and physics state; it adds no network field, authority path, damage change, or protocol bump. Losing aim, entering the player's view, dying, or moving behind cover clears that attacker's focus timer. Each attacker has a 5-second audio rearm window.

`DuelHud` draws a pulsing edge arc and chevron in the threat direction plus a `HIGH ALERT` plate. `RiftlineCombatFeedback` synthesizes a short local rising warning tone using the existing bounded audio pool. `riftline_arena.gd` evaluates both authoritative and replica opponents so the same behavior works offline, for a LAN host, and for a joining LAN client; resetting a match also clears stale chip state. A `--feedback-preview=high-alert-back` fixture provides repeatable visual capture without faking a gameplay or network event.

Merged forward onto the twelfth session's redesigned HUD: `_draw_high_alert()` was restyled onto that session's token/plate system (`HUD_ALERT`, `draw_plate`, `draw_tracked_centered`) in place of its original ad-hoc amber colour and plain rect, so the warning reads as this HUD's actual danger role rather than a fourth one-off hue - no behavioral change, same trigger logic, same public `show_high_alert()`/`clear_high_alert()` contract.

Verification: clean Godot 4.7.1 headless import; all 17 `tools/*_exercise.gd` scripts pass. The new exercise covers trigger timing, one-shot cue behavior while continuously targeted, range/aim rejection, behind-camera direction, physical cover suppression, and HUD set/clear state.

Not done: there is no chip inventory or loadout-selection UI yet, so every player receives High Alert automatically. A later chip-system pass can make it one mutually exclusive choice without changing this evaluator.
