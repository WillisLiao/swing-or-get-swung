# 2026-08-07 - Nuclear Rush: Robert's map becomes the only map, 4v4, realistic PBR

## What landed

**Robert's circular arena is now the game's only map.**
Ported forward from PR #1 (`robertwu072792`, branch `circular-nuke-arena`) rather than merged, because that branch predates two commits on `main`: the SUN/VOID to RED/BLUE rename and the lean removal.
His layout dimensions are preserved exactly - 60m radius floor, 40 tangent wall segments, base walls at z=+/-55 with side walls at x=+/-13, the 16-segment centre pickup room at radius 8.2, base cover pairs at z=+/-43, four cover columns per side at x=+/-32 (z=-24/-8/8/24), shoulder blocks at (+/-25, +/-34), three low round covers per side at z=+/-15, raised mid decks at z=+/-29 with ramps and supports, and roofed flank underpasses at x=+/-42.
His `_route_nodes` graph and lane posts were copied verbatim.
`RiftlineMap.Id` collapsed to the single value `CONCOURSE`; DUEL_YARD and all its geometry are gone.
RED holds north (+Z), BLUE holds south (-Z).

**Nuclear Rush is the only game mode**, implemented to `handoffs/DESIGN-nuclear-rush.md`.
Deathmatch and bomb defuse were deleted entirely: no `BombState`, no rounds, no side swaps, no intermission.
The core is carried to the carrying team's **own** pad - this is the correction to PR #1, which delivered to the enemy base.

Rules as built: 4v4, one continuous 600s clock, one core at centre, 2.5s install hold on your own pad, 25s launch countdown visible to both teams, 3s enemy hold-to-cancel, 1 point per successful launch only, first to 3 wins, higher score wins at expiry, tie enters **unbounded** sudden death, carrier death drops the core for either team, dropped core returns to centre after 15s, core respawns at centre 4s after a launch or cancel, carrier moves at 0.82x with full loadout, 3.0s respawn delay.

**The six open questions in the design doc are now decided** and recorded in it: movement-only carrier penalty (no sidearm lock), roles as playstyle labels only, 25s countdown visible to both teams, one fixed pad per base, hold-to-cancel matching the existing interact pattern, and unbounded sudden death.

That last one is also the deliberate fix for the stalemate tie-break raised in review on PR #1: the old `_finish_nuke_timeout()` fell through to `winner = nuke_carrier_team`, which resolved to whichever team the variable was initialised with when nobody had ever carried the core. An unbounded sudden death removes the accidental default rather than papering over it with a coin flip.

**Session layer is 4v4 and has nothing left to negotiate.**
`PROTOCOL_VERSION` 9 -> 10 (deliberate wire break). `RiftlineRoster.MAX_TEAM_SIZE` 5 -> 4, default team size 4. `map_id` and `game_mode` removed from the session descriptor, the lobby-state whitelist, and every adoption path; only display labels `MODE_LABEL`/`MAP_LABEL` remain, and they are never parsed back into an enum. `arena_id`, `arena_override_set`, `game_mode`, `default_arena_for_team_size()`, `arena_name()`, `arena_id_from_name()` all deleted. `RiftlineLobby.configure()` lost its `arena_id` parameter. Host capacity is now a single `APP_HOST_REMOTE_SLOTS := 7` so a full 4v4 including the host fits. The practice panel's DEATHMATCH/BOMB selector and its `mode_requested` signal are gone, remaining buttons re-flowed. `--mode=` and the map-override CLI options are gone.

**Realistic art direction, still with zero imported art.**
New `shaders/nuclear_pbr.gdshader`: real metal-roughness response (`diffuse_burley`, `specular_schlick_ggx`, no custom `light()`), procedural surface relief whose normal comes from an **analytic** sine-sum gradient, isotropic value-noise grime pooling in recesses, dust on upward faces, and material-supplied AO to substitute for the screen-space AO the Mobile renderer does not have.
The analytic gradient was chosen over noise-based normals specifically because perturbing from noise needs three extra samples per fragment at 120 Hz.
New `scripts/nuclear_materials.gd` (`NuclearMaterials`) is the single material factory - `concrete`, `metal`, `painted_metal`, `polymer`, `marking`, `emissive` - with parameter-keyed caching, because the arena builds hundreds of meshes and each distinct `ShaderMaterial` costs a uniform buffer.
Arena environment rebuilt: `ProceduralSkyMaterial` sky, `AMBIENT_SOURCE_SKY` + `REFLECTION_SOURCE_SKY` (the sky reflections are what make the PBR metal read as metal), `TONE_MAPPER_ACES`, glow, depth fog, adjustments, and a shadowed directional key with `SHADOW_PARALLEL_4_SPLITS` and a soft angular distance.
`project.godot` gained 4K/2K directional and positional shadow atlases, anisotropic filtering, 2x MSAA, and `force_vertex_shading=false`.

The renderer stays **Mobile**. SSAO, SSIL, SDFGI, SSR and volumetric fog are Forward+ only and are not affordable at 120 Hz on a phone; the arena is grepped clean of all five.

**Naming.** The game is **Swing or Get Swung**; `application/config/name` is the short form **SOGS** because iOS truncates a long label under the icon. Applied across `project.godot`, `export_presets.cfg`, `deploy.sh`, and the product-name exercise. Bundle id stays `com.lull.riftline` on purpose - changing it breaks code signing and orphans existing installs. The GitHub repo is now `WillisLiao/swing-or-get-swung`.

## Bug fixed: settings panel touch capture

Reported: pressing a settings slider and sliding - without lifting - onto another slider or button activated the other control and "messed up the whole settings".

Root cause was `duel_hud.gd`'s input dispatch replaying every `InputEventScreenDrag` as a fresh **press** (`_handle_settings_touch(index, position, true)`), so the handler re-ran its entire top-to-bottom hit test at the new position. Worse than the report suggested: the toggle chips ran `_aim_toggle = not _aim_toggle` on *every* drag event, flipping many times a second, and sliding off the panel edge closed the panel. `_settings_owner_touch` looked like it should have prevented this but tracks the combat HUD's settings *button*, not the control a settings touch has captured, and is normally -1 once the panel is open.

Fix: per-touch capture. `_settings_captures` maps a touch index to the control id it captured on press. `_settings_control_at()` is now the single place a point resolves to a control. Presses resolve once and apply; `_handle_settings_drag()` ignores any index without a capture and routes only to the captured id, where `_apply_settings_drag()` handles the three sliders and no-ops everything else; release erases only its own index. Tap-outside-to-close fires only for a press whose own down event landed outside. The mouse path routes identically.

`tools/riftline_settings_touch_exercise.gd` is a new regression test that drives real `InputEventScreenTouch`/`InputEventScreenDrag` objects through `_gui_input()`: 16 assertions covering the reported gesture, that sliders still drag their own control, that chips toggle exactly once per press and never per drag frame, that a captured drag leaving the panel does not close it, and that an unmatched touch index is ignored.

## Two other defects found by reviewing diffs rather than reports

- The rules rewrite deleted the `respawn_started` signal on the grounds that nothing referenced it. `riftline_arena.gd` connects it and the LAN host publishes remote `spawn` events from it, so respawns would have been invisible to every other device in the match. Restored.
- Objective audio was mapped to dead `objective_*`/`seed_*` event names, so all six Nuclear Rush events hit the dictionary default and silently played nothing. `riftline_combat_feedback.gd` now keys directly off the `core_picked_up`/`core_dropped`/`core_returned`/`core_installed`/`launch_cancelled`/`launch_complete` vocabulary, with `NUCLEAR_RUSH_EVENTS` keeping the audio bank and the dispatch check from drifting apart.

Also fixed during integration: `_offline_squad_size` was still being set to 5 at nine sites while `MAX_TEAM_SIZE` had become 4, so `roster.configure(5, ...)` would return false and the "full" practice drill would never build a roster. All sites now funnel through `_set_offline_squad_size()`, which clamps to the roster's range. And `rift_link_panel.gd:369` still referenced `RiftlineMap.Id.DUEL_YARD` in its lobby-state fixture; the dead `arena_id`/`arena_name` keys were dropped and its squad preview is now 4v4.

## Known remaining work

- **Bots have no objective awareness.** `bot_duelist.gd` is combat-only; `set_objective_context()` exists and the arena feeds it, but nothing consumes it yet. Bots will not fetch the core, deliver it, defend a launch, or raid to cancel, which limits offline and bot-filled 4v4.
- **Characters and weapons still use `pulp_lit`.** `duelist.gd` was not converted to `NuclearMaterials`, so players read as flat blocky silhouettes against PBR level geometry.
- **Surface relief still bands on large flat areas.** The sine-sum albedo modulation produces visible coherent wavefronts on the floor and large walls. Deferred by request; the fix is to drive albedo from the isotropic value noise and leave the sine sum for the normal only.
- **Palette is over-saturated for a realistic target.** Team accents are applied to whole base walls, and the launch-pad emissive ring is hot enough to blow out.
- **Touch playtest on device still outstanding** for the Nuclear Rush install/cancel flow, which is what review on PR #1 asked for.
