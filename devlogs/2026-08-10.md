# Devlog - 2026-08-10

## Symmetric centre-facing overlook cover

The user requested a player-height wall on the centre-facing edge of the elevated overlook, then identified that the existing left and right parapets were not identical.

Concourse layout V6 adds `OverlookCenterCover` for both teams at team-local position `(22, 4.1, 26)` with dimensions `(1.2, 1.8, 8.0)`. The wall joins the two existing low parapets but stops before either stair mouth. The north inner parapet was corrected from a 5.0 m span centred at local z=32.5 to a 4.0 m span centred at z=32.0, exactly mirroring the south parapet around the new centre cover. Both stair approaches now have the same 1.75 m clearance.

The layout changes from 126 to 128 solids and the network protocol moves from 16 to 17 so clients with unequal geometry cannot silently pair with layout-V6 clients.

Blender MCP edited `art/maps/concourse_v2_access_redesign.blend` without adding materials or mesh roles. The wall bodies and ribs use `STRUCT_Gunmetal`, their cap and lower band use `SYSTEMS_DarkSteel`, and four restrained markers use `LIGHT_Amber`. The unequal north parapet components in those same role meshes were replaced with translated copies of the south components. The source remains nine merged role meshes with one surface each, and the exported `assets/maps/concourse_v2_environment.glb` is 1,365,488 bytes and 25,992 triangles. Recoverable pre-edit files are `/tmp/concourse_v2_access_before_center_cover.blend` and `/tmp/concourse_v2_access_before_parapet_symmetry.blend`.

`tools/riftline_map_exercise.gd` now asserts both centre-cover records and the exact position and dimensions of every inner parapet. `tools/riftline_map_visual_exercise.gd` tracks the revised triangle contract.

Validation completed through both required live MCP connections. Godot reimported the GLB cleanly; all 19 exercise scripts passed. A live main-scene run contained 128 static gameplay solids plus the visual wrapper, including `RedOverlookCenterCover` and `BlueOverlookCenterCover` and all four symmetric parapets. Blender MCP reported 25,992 triangles and a viewport review confirmed matching geometry on both sides. The headless 4v4 CPU sample printed `frames=120 avg_ms=16.67 min_ms=16.67 max_ms=16.67`; its game process did not self-exit and was stopped by the 60-second watchdog after the sample, so it is not mobile GPU evidence. No phone or tablet was launched or deployed.

## Reference-style second-floor platform redesign

The user supplied a reference showing a broad elevated deck with two stair approaches, low perimeter rails, one tall offset divider, staggered waist-high cover and a wide framed exit. The previous V6 overlook still read as a boxed corridor because its full-height outer wall and paired cross-baffle wings dominated the entire 10 by 28 m deck.

Layout V7 removes `OverlookOuterWall` and every `OverlookBaffle` wing. Each team now receives `OverlookOuterRail`, a 5 m-wide divider rotated 12 degrees, two 3.2 m-wide staggered cover blocks, and a portal formed by two posts plus an overhead header. The prior centre-facing 1.8 m cover and its symmetric low parapets remain. The cover arrangement produces a shallow slalom: outer side past the divider, inner side around the south cover, then outer side around the north cover and through the 7.2 m-clear portal. The two 4.5 m-wide stairs remain unchanged.

`RiftlineMapLayout.VERSION` moves from 6 to 7, the solid count from 128 to 132, and `RiftlineNetwork.PROTOCOL_VERSION` from 17 to 18. `tools/riftline_map_exercise.gd` asserts all seven new platform records for both teams, rejects the old boxed-corridor names and drives a 0.9 m-wide, 1.8 m-high CharacterBody probe through every turn of both mirrored decks.

Blender MCP removed only the disconnected components inside the two overlook regions, then rebuilt the platforms inside the existing nine role meshes. The safety rail is an open metal frame with continuous top and base beams, the divider and cover receive dark caps and amber guidance, and the portal uses lime vertical blades plus one white header light. No texture, new material, light object, imported mesh or draw-call role was introduced. `art/maps/concourse_v2_access_redesign.blend` contains nine identity-transform mesh objects, one material each and 24,120 triangles; `assets/maps/concourse_v2_environment.glb` exports the same nine primitives at 1,611,644 bytes. The pre-edit backup is `/tmp/concourse_v2_access_before_reference_overlook.blend`.

Godot reimport initially retained the old editor cache, so an independent headless import was run and visibly reimported both the `.blend` and `.glb`. The final Concourse visual exercise then passed at exactly 24,120 imported triangles and confirmed that all 132 collision solids have authored geometry. All 19 exercise scripts pass. Live Godot MCP loaded 133 map children: 132 static solids plus the visual wrapper, including all 26 RED/BLUE overlook nodes at the expected mirrored positions and rotations. Blender MCP's close view and Godot MCP's cinematic preview were inspected against the reference.

The final headless 4v4 sample printed `frames=120 avg_ms=16.67 min_ms=16.67 max_ms=16.67`; as before, the non-exiting game process was stopped by the watchdog after printing the sample. This change adds only static, already-merged geometry and collision and reduces the shell by 1,872 triangles, with no per-frame or per-physics-tick work. Headless is CPU-only and no mobile GPU, human touch, thermal, battery or long-session result is claimed. No phone or tablet was launched or deployed.
