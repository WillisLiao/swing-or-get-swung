The user rejected the V7 platform because its staggered cover slalom did not match the supplied screenshot, then clarified that the edge facing the nuclear-objective area must be glass and that the screenshot itself, not a loose inspiration image, is authoritative. The first reconstruction exposed a coordinate-system mistake: Blender +Y imports as Godot -Z, so the candidate pieces landed in the wrong quadrants and interfered with the maintenance visual. The source was restored from `/tmp/concourse_v2_before_reference_fidelity_fix.blend`, and the two regions were rebuilt in their correct Blender quadrants: RED at +X/-Y and BLUE at -X/+Y. `GLASS_Ballistic` is a new tenth merged mesh role. At runtime it receives one cached two-sided transparent `StandardMaterial3D` with alpha 0.26, metallic 0.05 and roughness 0.18. The final Blender source is 359,102 bytes; the exported GLB is 1,284,696 bytes with ten mesh roles and 24,480 triangles.
